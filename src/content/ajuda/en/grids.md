Grids builds a **piece's structure** (margins, columns, rows, baseline) and delivers it ready for layout in InDesign or Affinity Publisher. You see the result in the preview, live, while you adjust.

{marca: Opened from inside a brand, the file also comes with the **brand's logo, colors and text styles** already in place.}

## Step 1 · The format

Choose the piece's size from the list. It comes in four groups: **Print**, **Social, internal marketing and employer branding**, **Digital media** and **Presentation**. Below the list, the screen says where the measurement comes from.

- For **printed** pieces, measurements are in millimeters; for digital ones, in pixels.
- **Book:** **Spine** and **Flap** appear. The spine depends on the paper and the number of pages, so confirm the measurement with the printer.
- When the piece has a **safe area** (for example, a social network's), it appears in the preview, in red, with the note below.

## Step 2 · The grid type

Choose one of the four:

| Type | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Manuscript** | a single block of text, as in a book |
| **Columns** | columns and gutter (the space between them) |
| **Modular** | columns × rows, forming modules |
| **Hierarchical** | zones defined by the content |

Then adjust what the type asks for:

- **Columns**, **Rows** and **Gutter**;
- **Baseline**, the ruling that aligns the text (in points, for print);
- **Margins** (top, bottom, left, right);
- in **Manuscript**, the **margin proportion** (Brennimark's proposal, the classic book 2 : 3 : 4 : 6, the Van de Graaf canon or the golden section);
- in **Hierarchical**, the **zone split** (1 : 2, 2 : 3, golden and others).

The starting numbers are **Brennimark's proposal**. The screen warns you if the margins get too large for the piece to fit.

## Step 3 · Deconstruction (optional)

For freer layouts. It doesn't appear for books.

- **Overlaid grid:** a second grid on top of the first, with the columns you choose.
- **Rotated grid:** the grid turned at an angle, from −45° to 45°.

In InDesign, each one comes on its **own non-printing layer**. Turn them on and off in the **Layers** panel.

:::marca
## The brand in the document

Between Step 3 and the download, the block with the brand's name appears.

- **Grid rule for this format.** Brennimark reads the manual and shows the grid the brand defined for the chosen format, with the page. If the manual doesn't define one, Brennimark's proposal applies.
  - **A draft doesn't apply silently.** Whoever has permission checks it against the manual and clicks **Approve this grid** (or **Approve the N grids**, all at once).
  - If the manual carries **several grids** for the same format, choose which to use under **Brand grid**.
  - Adjusted the measurements and want that to apply? **Save this grid as the rule for this format**. It applies only to this format and stays in draft until someone approves it.
- **Logo.** It comes from the brand's **Materials** (PDF, AI, SVG, PNG or JPG). Choose the version and the **corner** it goes in. Grids shows the logo's **clear space**; if it doesn't fit within the margins, click **Fit the margins to the clear space**. With no logo in those formats, upload one in Materials.
- **Colors.** Only **approved** colors come in: the manual's CMYK for printed pieces, RGB for digital ones. Colors in draft are left out.
- **Text styles.** They come from the brand's typography. Without an approved font, the styles come out at the grid's size and leading, with InDesign's default font.
:::

## Step 4 · Download

- **Download for InDesign (.idml):** the file with the layered grid, ready for layout. It also opens in **Affinity Publisher**. {marca: It comes in a **.zip** with the .idml, the **Links** folder (with the logo) and a **README**. Keep the Links folder next to the file.}
- **Reference PDF:** the grid as a PDF, for other programs.
- **Back to Brennimark's proposal** undoes your adjustments. {marca: Inside a brand, the button is called **Back to the brand grid**.}
