From zero to your first manual online, with the team inside. It takes a few minutes.

## 1. Sign in to your account

The agency account is created when the subscription is confirmed. Sign in at **brennimark.com/login** with the email you used to buy. The first time, Brennimark asks for your name and the agency's name.

> If someone invited you: whoever invited you hands you a **temporary password**. Sign in with it and Brennimark asks you to create your own.

## 2. Bring in the manual as a PDF

In the left menu, choose **Import a manual** and then **Choose a PDF**.

- Brennimark reads the file and shows a **preview**. Until you confirm, **nothing is saved**.
- Check the **manual's language** (Portuguese or English).
- Under **Features for this brand**, tick what this brand will use. The choice is yours; the PDF doesn't decide for you.
- When everything looks right, click **Create the brand with these drafts**.

Very large manuals may exceed the installation's limit. When that happens, the message says whether it was the product's limit or the current installation's.

## 3. Let Vini read the pages

After the brand is created, Brennimark offers to prepare an image of each page, so Vini can read tables and visual pages. It takes a few minutes. You can **skip and prepare later**, in the manual.

## 4. Review what the AI read

What Brennimark extracts from the PDF (colors, typography, grids, rules) comes in as a **draft**. A draft doesn't count as a rule: Vini cites it, but says it is a draft. To become a rule, someone with permission has to **approve** it.

Approval happens on each subject's own screen, with buttons such as **Approve this grid** in Grids. Review and approve what is right. What stays unapproved remains visible, labeled as a draft.

## 5. Upload the brand materials

In **Materials**, upload logos, icons and other working files. Whoever consults the brand downloads from here. If the brand uses a licensed font, font hosting requires your acceptance of the license agreement before it is switched on.

## 6. Invite the team

In **People**, use **Grant access**:

| Level | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Consult** | Reads the manual and downloads the materials, only for the brands you choose. |
| **Administers the account** | Reaches every brand, including ones created later, and grants access to other people. |

Choose the level, choose the brands (for Consult, at least one) and confirm. Brennimark shows a **temporary password**: copy it and give it to the person. The row stays at *Waiting for first sign-in* until they sign in. For details, such as generating another password or removing access, see [People and access](/ajuda/pessoas-e-acesso).

## 7. Send to people without an account

For a supplier or client who only needs a few files, use **Delivery links** and then **New delivery link**. The link has a deadline, and the address appears **only once**: copy it right away.

## 8. Try Vini

Vini's button sits in the corner of the screen. Ask anything about the brand: Vini answers citing the manual's page. Vini also checks whether a piece follows the manual.
