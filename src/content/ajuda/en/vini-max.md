Vini is the brand's assistant. Vini answers based on **that brand's manual**, shows the page each
answer came from and says when something isn't documented. The decision stays yours.

## Open and close

The **Ask Vini** button sits in the corner of the screen, on any page of the brand. Click it to open the
window. **Collapse** closes it, and the conversation stays where it was.

In brands where the conversation is turned off, the window says *In this brand, the conversation isn't
available*. Whoever administers the brand decides that.

## Ask about the brand

Write the question and press Enter. Ask about color, typography, logo, tone of voice, whatever you need.

- **The answer cites the page.** Click the citation (for example, *[P. 12]*) and the manual opens there.
- **Vini states the status of what it cites:** an **approved** rule, or a **draft** (read from the manual, not yet
  confirmed). A draft is offered, but labeled as such.
- **When there is no guideline, Vini says there isn't.** Vini doesn't make up rules. "No guideline
  documented" is an answer, and it is better than leading you to get it wrong in front of the client.
- **Stop** halts the answer without losing the question. If the answer comes out **incomplete**, click
  **Ask again**.

## Analyze a piece

To check whether a piece follows the manual:

1. Drag the piece into the window, or use the **paperclip** in the footer. It accepts **JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to
   10 MB**.
2. If you want, choose the **type of piece** and write **What should be checked?** (optional): for example, "only the
   logo use".
3. Vini shows the progress: **Preparing the piece**, **Consulting the visual AI** and **Checking against
   the manual**.

The result brings a badge:

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Aligned** | The piece follows what the manual documents. |
| **Review needed** | Part follows and part needs adjusting. |
| **Misaligned** | The piece departs from the manual. |
| **No verdict** | Vini couldn't conclude. |

Below the badge come the details, with the manual page for each point. Click **Download the image with
the corrections** to take the piece with the annotations. After the analysis, you can **ask about the
piece** or **choose another piece**.

The result is kept in the brand's history.

## Generate a prompt with the brand's DNA

To ask another AI for an image or text while keeping the brand:

1. Choose **Build a prompt with the brand's DNA**.
2. Write **what you are going to create**: for example, "product photo of the laptop for Instagram, light
   background".
3. Click **See the rules**. Vini searches the manual for what governs it.
4. Under **Go into the prompt — approved**, are the approved rules. They go in on their own.
5. Under **Draft — tick to include**, are the ones not yet approved. **Nothing goes in
   silently:** tick only what you want.
6. Click **Build prompt** and then **Copy prompt**. The screen shows **Built from:**,
   with the rules used.

If the prompt uses a draft rule, the screen warns: *the brand hasn't approved it yet; check before
taking the piece to the client.*

## The usage limit

Vini has a **daily** and a **monthly** limit per account. Whoever administers sees how much has been used under
**Settings**, in **Usage**. Near the limit, the screen warns. **At 100%, Vini refuses
new requests until it renews:** the daily limit renews at 0:00 UTC (9 p.m. in Brasília) and the monthly one on the 1st.
Only Vini stops; the rest of the platform keeps working. Details in [Subscription](/ajuda/assinatura).

## Go back to a conversation

Under **Conversations**, the earlier conversations for this brand are kept. Open one to continue, or delete it.
They are **yours**: **nobody reads your conversation, not even whoever administers the account.** (Checked in the database: the access rule compares the conversation's author.)
