Whoever administers the account decides who gets in and what each person can do. This screen only appears for administrators.

## The two access levels

| Level | What they can do | What they reach |
|---|---|---|
| **Consult** | Reads the manual and downloads the materials. | Only the brands you grant, one by one. |
| **Administers the account** | Everything Consult does, plus granting and removing access. | Every brand in the account, including ones created later. |

A person can be in more than one account. Access in one doesn't show up in the other.

## Give someone access

1. Under **People**, fill in the **name** and the **email**.
2. Choose the level: **Consult — reads and downloads** or **Administers the account**.
3. For Consult, tick **at least one brand**. For people who administer, it isn't needed: they reach all of them.
4. Click **Grant access**.

**No email is sent.** Brennimark shows a **temporary password, only once**. Copy it with **Copy** and hand it to the person yourself (by message, for example). Then click **I've handed it over**.

The person signs in with the email and this password, and **chooses their own password on first sign-in**. Only then does access begin.

The temporary password is valid for **72 hours**.

## Follow who has signed in

The list shows, for each person, the **level** and the **brands they reach**. Until they sign in for the first time, **Waiting for first sign-in** appears, with the date until which the password is valid. After the deadline, **Temporary password expired** appears.

## The password didn't arrive, or expired

While the person hasn't signed in yet, click **Generate new password**. The previous one stops working, and the new one appears only once, as the first did.

## Remove access

- **Remove [brand]** (next to the brand's name) removes only that brand. The person keeps the others.
- **Remove access** removes **all** the person's access **in this account**.

Brennimark asks for confirmation first. Removing access doesn't delete anything the person downloaded earlier: what is already on their computer stays there. What they did is kept in the **Records** (who accessed, downloaded and changed what), and the removal appears there as *revoked*.

## Give access to someone without an account

If the person only needs to receive a few files (a supplier, for example), you don't need to give access: use **Delivery links**, with a deadline. See [Getting started](/ajuda/primeiros-passos), step 7.
