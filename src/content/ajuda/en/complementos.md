The manual doesn't cover everything. A **supplement** is a text the brand writes for what the manual doesn't
say. **The manual remains the reference:** the supplement adds to it, and Vini cites both when they
speak about the same subject.

They live in the **Supplements** tab, in the brand's top bar.

## Write a supplement

Whoever edits the brand:

1. Click **+ New supplement**.
2. Give it a **title** (for example, *Symbol over photography*).
3. Write the **text**, in Markdown. Use `##` to divide it into sections: **each section becomes an excerpt that
   Vini cites**.
4. See the result in the **Preview**, alongside.
5. Click **Save draft**.

## Draft and published

- **Draft:** *only editors see it*. Vini does **not** use it.
- **Publish:** after **Confirm**, **everyone who reaches the brand can read it**, and Vini starts to
  use this text in its answers.

## Change a supplement that is already published

Click **Edit**. The change is kept as a **draft edit**, and **the published text keeps
applying** until you publish the edit. To give up, **Discard the edit**.

## Archive and reactivate

- **Archive:** the supplement **leaves the reading view and Vini**. The text and the history stay saved.
- **Reactivate:** brings it back.
- **Discard draft:** only for what **was never published**, and it disappears entirely.

## History

Click **History** to see the versions: what was **published**, **archived** or **reactivated**, and
when.
