The brand's manual is the very PDF the studio laid out, page by page, just as it was made. On top of it sit the index, the search and the download.

## Open the manual

Choose the brand and click **Manual** in the top bar. The pages appear one below the other: scroll to read.

## Go straight to a page

- **In the footer**, type the number in the page field (it shows *12 of 47*) and press Enter.
- **Through the Contents**, below.
- **Through Vini:** when Vini cites a page (for example, *[P. 12]*), click the citation and the manual opens there.

## Use the contents

Click **Contents**. The list takes you to the chapter.

- **Filter chapters** searches by name, for long manuals.
- When the PDF carries its own table of contents, the list says *The document's own table of contents*. When it doesn't, Brennimark proposes one and says *Contents proposed by the extraction*: check it before trusting it.

## Search for a word

Type in **Search** and press Enter. The manual goes to the first page where the word appears; Enter again goes to the next. If there is none, the screen says **Not found**.

Search reads the text of the pages. If a page is only an image (a photo of a table, for example), search won't find it.

## Adjust the size

- **− and +** decrease and increase the zoom.
- The number in the middle opens the list: **Fit to width** or a value from 50% to 300%.
- In the footer, the zoom appears as a percentage, or as *fit* when it is at screen width.

## Thumbnails and full screen

Under **•••** (More manual actions):

- **Show thumbnails** opens a strip with all the pages, on the left. Click to go to one.
- **Full screen** leaves only the manual.

## Download the PDF

Under **•••**, click **Download the PDF**. Anyone with access to the brand can download. The download is **recorded** (who downloaded and when), and appears in the account's Records.

## For people who edit the brand

These items only appear for people who can edit the brand.

**Prepare the manual for Vini.** Under **•••**, click **Prepare the manual for Vini (N pages)**. It generates an image of each page, so Vini can read tables and visual pages. It takes a few minutes, and can be done after the import. When it finishes, the item becomes **Manual ready for Vini ✓**. If a page fails, it becomes **Try again**.

**Pending registration.** If the **Source document registration pending** notice appears, the manual stays open and can be read normally. What is missing is the page-by-page registration (measurements, coverage and provenance), which search and curation use. Click **Finish registration** to complete it.

To bring in a new manual, see [Getting started](/ajuda/primeiros-passos), step 2.
