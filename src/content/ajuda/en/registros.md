Records tell you **who did what and when** in the account. They are for checking who downloaded a file,
who was given access and what changed. It is a screen for whoever administers the account, under **Records**, in the
left menu.

## The three tabs

- **Downloads:** who downloaded what. *Download started* means the person received a valid
  address for the file at that moment. **It doesn't mean the transfer finished.**
- **Accesses:** who **gave, changed or removed** whose access. *Visits to brands are not recorded.*
- **Actions:** changes to the **manual's content**: versions published and restored. Changes to
  Materials and to the Palette **don't appear here yet**.

There is a fourth, **Unanswered questions**, marked *Coming later*. It will show what people
ask Vini that the manual doesn't answer (the bridge to Supplements). Conversations belong only to whoever
asks, so the report will be **aggregated**, and only appears when enough people ask the same
thing, so nobody is identified by their question.

## Filter

Use the filters at the top of the list:

- **Brand:** *All brands* or just one.
- **Person:** name or email.
- **From** and **To:** the period.

Click **Filter**. The list brings **When**, **Brand**, **Person** and **What**. To see more
rows, **Load more**. If nothing matches the filters, the screen says *Nothing recorded with these
filters*.

## What Records are not

Records are **append-only**: what happened can't be edited or deleted through the screen. They tell what
the platform saw, and that is why visits and Vini's conversations are left out.
