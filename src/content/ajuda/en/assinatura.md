Everything about the plan, the payment and what is yours lives in **Settings**, in the left menu. This
area is for whoever administers the account.

## See the plan

Under **Plan**, you see the **plan name**, how many **brands** the account uses (for example, *3 of 5*) and
the date until which it is **paid**. If the account has reached its brand limit, the screen says so, and to add more
you change plan.

## Pay, change plan and cancel

Click **Manage subscription**. You go to **Stripe**'s secure page, where the **card**, the **plan**, the
**cancellation** and the **invoices** live.

- Payment is **by credit card only**, and the subscription is **monthly, renewing
  automatically** on the same day it was taken out.
- Brennimark **does not receive or store your card number**.

## Cancel

Cancel under **Manage subscription**. The cancellation is confirmed by email right away.

- **Full access continues until the end of the period already paid.** Under **Plan**, the *Paid until* field becomes
  *Ends on*.
- Changed your mind before the end? **You can undo it** on the same subscription page.

**Right of withdrawal:** anyone who cancels within **7 days** of first subscribing gets the amount
paid back in full, on the same card. Because the amount is returned in full, full access **ends
immediately** and the account becomes **read-only**, like any cancelled account. The right of withdrawal applies only
to an email's **first subscription**, and not to cancellation for non-payment.

## If the payment fails

If a charge doesn't go through, the platform notifies the account owner and **everything keeps working for 7
days**. After that period without it being settled, the account becomes **read-only**:

- **Keeps working:** consulting and downloading.
- **Stops:** Vini, editing and new brands.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Settle the payment and everything comes back.

## Follow usage

Under **Usage**, you see:

- **Vini's limit**: how much of the **daily** and **monthly** limit has been used. At 100%, Vini
  refuses new requests until it renews (0:00 UTC, which is 9 p.m. in Brasília, and on the 1st for the monthly one);
- the **pieces analyzed** this month;
- the **space each brand takes up**.

## Export everything that is yours

Under **Export everything**, click **Request the export**. You receive a copy of everything that is yours: the
**original files of each brand** and an **index of the content**. The browser saves **one .zip per
brand**, and you need to **keep the tab open** until it finishes.

If it doesn't work, ask on the screen itself: we deliver within **15 days**.

## After cancelling

The account is kept, **read-only, for 12 months**, so you can come back or export what is
yours. **We warn you 30 days before** the end of that period. After it, the content is deleted
permanently: the brands, the files and the access. Export before, or ask support for deletion before the deadline if you
prefer. Only the records the law requires us to keep remain (such as tax data).

## Account opened by the team

If the account was opened by the Brennimark team, it **has no online subscription**, and the screen says so.
For anything about the plan, talk to us through [support](/ajuda#suporte).
