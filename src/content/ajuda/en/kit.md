You upload the logo once and Kit delivers, in one click, **every everyday size**: web, social networks, internal marketing and employer branding, email and presentations. Everything is done **on your computer**.

## Step 1 · The logo

:::site
- **Logotype:** drag the file or click to choose. **SVG, PDF or AI** give the best result; PNG works from 1024 px.
- **Brand name:** Kit tries to fill it in from the file name. Correct it if needed.
- **Have a vertical version, a symbol or a negative version?** Open and upload them, if you have them:
  - **Vertical logotype:** used in the squares when there is no symbol;
  - **Symbol:** used in icons and profiles;
  - **Version for dark backgrounds:** if it is missing, Kit makes the logo white by itself.
:::

:::marca
The logo comes from the brand's **Materials**. If there is more than one version, choose which to use, with thumbnails. Without the logotype in Materials (positive version, as SVG, PDF, AI or PNG), Kit waits: as soon as someone registers it, it builds every size.
:::

## Step 2 · Adjustments

- **Background:** **White**, **Dark**, **Brand color** or **Transparent**. {marca: Under *Brand color*, you choose among the palette's **approved** colors.} On social profiles, the transparent background gets whatever the network picks (white or black).
- **Margin:** the free space around the logo, inside each image, from 4% to 30%.
- **Use the symbol in icons and profiles:** appears when there is a symbol.
- **Brand rules.** There are two, and they are in the manual, on the clear-space and minimum-size pages:
  - **Clear space:** the free margin around the logo, as a percentage of its height;
  - **Minimum size:** the smallest width, in px, at which the logo may appear (one for the logotype, another for the symbol).

:::site
Type in what the manual defines and **leave blank what it doesn't define**.
:::

:::marca
Kit **reads the manual by itself** and shows the value, the page and the status (*approved*, *read by the AI and not yet confirmed* or *not yet approved*). Whoever has permission checks it against the manual and **confirms**, or **corrects** the value and the page. **Only the approved rule applies without a warning.**
:::

If any file ends up **below the minimum size**, Kit warns you and marks it in the list. For small icons, a separate symbol usually solves it.

## Step 3 · Download

- **Download everything (.zip):** the whole kit, organized by folder. The button shows how many files come.
- **Only the open group:** downloads just the files of the open tab.
- The package carries a **README**. {marca: It says which **page of the manual** each rule came from.}

## The preview: one group per tab

On the right, each tab shows the files for that destination **in the place where they will be used**:

| Tab | What it has |
|---|---|
| **Web** | favicons (16, 32 and 48), iPhone and Android icons, link image |
| **Social networks** | profiles, logos and covers (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, WhatsApp Business) |
| **Internal marketing and employer branding** | LinkedIn (showcase, Life tab, event), Gupy, Glassdoor, Viva Engage, Teams, Slack, Google Workspace, video-call background, wallpaper |
| **Email** | the signature logo and the signature ready to copy |
| **Presentation** | horizontal logotype, vertical, symbol only, for dark backgrounds and in one color; avatar for Slack, Teams and Google |

## The email signature

In the **Email** tab:

1. Fill in **Name**, **Job title**, **Phone** and **Website**.
2. The **logo address** is optional, but it is needed for the logo to show up for whoever receives it: upload the file `assinatura-logo-600.png` to the company's website and paste the address.
3. Click **Copy signature** and paste it:
   - **Gmail:** Settings → See all settings → Signature
   - **Outlook:** Settings → Accounts → Signatures
   - **Apple Mail:** Settings → Signatures (untick “always match my default message font”)

{marca: The signature uses the **approved font** from the brand's typography (the substitute for the "text" use), and the screen says which one.}
