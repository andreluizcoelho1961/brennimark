Colors measures any color and tells you what to do with it: the codes, whether text can be read over it and, inside a brand, whether it is the brand's color.

## 1. Pick a color

Use one of these three ways:

- **Eyedropper on screen:** click it and then the color, anywhere on the screen, including in other programs. It only appears in browsers that allow it; for the others, see below.
- **Upload an image:** send a photo or a screenshot and click the color you want to measure. You can also **paste a screenshot** straight into the page (⌘V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
- **Type the code:** HEX (`#FF4103`), RGB (`255 65 3`), HSB or CMYK (`cmyk(0 85 100 0)`). Click **Use**.

**On a Mac, with Safari or Firefox:** the button becomes **Mac eyedropper**. Click it, then **Show Colors…** in the panel that opens, and the **magnifier**. Then click the color, anywhere on the screen.

## 2. Read the codes

HEX, RGB, HSB and CMYK appear ready. Each one has a **Copy** button.

- **CMYK is approximate.** Choose **coated** (coated paper) or **offset** (uncoated) for the conversion. For print, the manual's CMYK or the printer's proof is what counts.
- **Colors doesn't suggest Pantone.** The reference is Pantone's physical guide. {marca: If the manual declares a Pantone, it appears, with the page.}

## 3. See whether it can be read

Under **Is it readable?**, choose the text color (white, black, or a brand color or one of the last ones you picked). Colors shows:

- the **contrast ratio** (for example, 4.6 : 1);
- the verdict by the **WCAG** accessibility standard: **small text** and **large text** get **AAA** (excellent), **AA** (good) or **fails**; **icons and buttons** get **passes** or **fails**;
- how the color looks to people with **color blindness**, in the three types: **protanopia** (no red), **deuteranopia** (no green) and **tritanopia** (no blue).

:::marca
## 4. Is it the brand's color?

Under **Is it the brand's color?**, Colors compares the color with the palette and answers in one sentence:

- **It is [color], approved:** it is exactly a palette color;
- **Practically [color]:** the difference is imperceptible to the eye;
- **Similar to [color]** or **Not a brand color** (with the closest one).

When the color comes from the manual, **See in the manual, p. N** appears. Click a palette color to measure it. A color in **draft** has not been approved yet and **does not count as a brand color**.
:::

## 5. Save and take the colors with you

- **Picked colors:** the last 12 are kept, **only in this browser**. If you switch computers or clear the browser's data, they disappear.
- **Take away:** download the colors as **ASE** (palette for Adobe programs), **CSS**, **JSON** or **PNG**, or use **Copy list**. {marca: With a brand open, the file carries the brand's **approved palette**.}
