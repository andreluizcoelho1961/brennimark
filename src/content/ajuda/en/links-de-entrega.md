A delivery link sends **chosen files** to people who don't have a Brennimark account: the printer
for a job, a supplier, a client. The link has a **deadline** and **records who downloaded**.

This screen is for whoever administers the account, under **Delivery links**, in the left menu.

## Create a link

1. Click **New delivery link**.
2. Give the link a **name** (for example, *Pampa Print Shop — October posters*).
3. If you want, fill in **For whom** (optional).
4. Choose the **brand**.
5. Set the **deadline in days**: from 1 to 30. The default is 7.
6. Tick the **files**, up to 60. They come from the brand's **Materials**, only the ones in use.
7. Create the link.

**The address appears only once.** Copy it right away with **Copy** and send it to whoever will receive it. For
security, Brennimark **does not store the address**, only a digest that doesn't reveal it. If it gets lost,
**close the link and create another**.

## What goes by link and what doesn't

- **Files from Materials:** they go. If you **replace** a file later, the link delivers the
  **new version**.
- **Fonts:** **don't go by link.** Only people with access to the brand receive them, because of the license.
- A file the brand has **taken out of use** stops being available, even inside a link.

## What the person sees

When opening the link, the person sees the brand's name and the list of files, with the **manual's rule** for
each group (for example, how to use the logo). To download, they **say who they are**: name and email. Then,
**Download the file** or **Download the selected one**.

They don't need an account. Name and email serve only for the record of who downloaded, and **are not
verified**: they are what the person typed.

## Follow what happened

The list shows each link, with **Created**, **Deadline**, **Status** and how many **downloads** it had (with the
latest one). The status is one of these:

- **Active:** within the deadline and open.
- **Expired:** the deadline has passed.
- **Closed:** you closed it early.

Click **Accesses** to see who downloaded what and when. If nobody has opened it, the screen says *Nobody
has opened this link yet*.

## Close a link

Click **Close** and confirm with **Close now**. The link stops working immediately. Whoever
tries to open it sees that it was closed and is told to talk to whoever sent it.

## When the person can't open it

| The person sees | What happened |
|---|---|
| *This link has expired* | The deadline has passed. Create another. |
| *This link was closed* | You closed it. |
| *This link doesn't exist* | The address arrived incomplete. Ask them to check it came in full. |
